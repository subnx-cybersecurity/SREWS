Sri Rakesh Educational Welfare Society (SREWS) is a Non- Governmental organization registered under Societies Registration act XX1 of 1860 with registration number 108/84, with the major object of education. For a period of decade it was engaged in running education institutions.
During 1994 then executive committee adopted a resolution by incorporating some by laws to take welfare activities and governs essential programs for Health, Forest and rural development programs and got sanctioned and executed the said programs detailed below. The organization is retaining the programs detailed below since the Couple of decades to till date.
SREWS is a Non Profit Organization that implements a Portfolio of Social Development & Environment projects.
